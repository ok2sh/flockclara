    1.     Provide a detailed description of the product/ service.
           The Flock Safety ALPR Camera System will function to automatically capture an image of a vehicle and the
           vehicle' s license plate, transform the plate image into alphanumeric characters using optical character
           recognition,          compare   the plate number   acquired    to one or more databases(   also   known as" hot lists") of
           vehicles of interest to law enforcement, and then alert law enforcement officers when a vehicle of interest has
           been observed.
    2.     Provide a clear statement for the need including any unique qualifications, certifications, or expertise
           requirements. (Please provide documentation if available)
           Traditional ALPR systems include fixed camera locations or mobile devices which SCPD has currently
           installed on four patrol cars. The Flock ALPR Camera System will consist of 12 fixed cameras that will be
           installed in high crime areas. These cameras are capable of reading two lanes of traffic at once, operate over
           wireless network with cloud storage, and download data directly to the Northern California Regional
           Intelligence Center. They are also solar powered and come in a small, self-contained package that can easily
           be mounted on existing light poles.
    3.     Description of the unique nature of the product/ service that prohibits competition( what is unique
           about this procurement to justify award of contract without engaging in a standard procurement
           process?).
           SCPD wants to conduct a pilot program to determine if cameras installed in select areas will reduce crime in
           the city. If the cameras prove to be effective, then SCPD will work with Purchasing to determine the
           permanent solution which may include conducting a competitive process. Under Santa Clara City Code
           Section 2. 105. 160, a sole source procurement is allowed for the purpose of conducting a pilot program.
